# Product Success Metrics: AgencyOS

We track these metrics to ensure that AgencyOS is delivering tangible business value and maintaining a sustainable growth trajectory.

## 🔭 North Star Metric
**Time-to-Invoice (TTI)**: The average time cumulative hours spent by an agency on manual billing each month. Our goal is to reduce this by 80% through automated invoicing.

## 📊 Business Growth Metrics
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Total subscription revenue across Free, Pro, and Enterprise tiers.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: The investment required to sign one new agency organization.
- **Churn Rate**: The percentage of agency organizations that cancel their subscription annually.

## ⚙️ Operational Efficiency Metrics
- **Administrative Overhead**: Percentage of total user time spent on "Management" tasks vs "Execution" tasks.
- **Task Prioritization Drift**: The delta between RICE-prioritized tasks and actual task completion order.
- **Resource Utilization**: Ratio of team capacity to assigned work items (Aiming for 85% optimization).

## 💡 Feature Adoption Metrics
- **Invoice Automation Engagement**: Percentage of users who generate invoices via the automated engine vs manual entry.
- **Predictive Forecast Accuracy**: The variance between AI-predicted cash flow and actual realized cash flow.

---
*Success is measured by the hours we put back into the hands of agency owners.*
